    PS5 storage for modders

    Skyrim's mod space on PS5 is its own 15 GB pool (Bethesda patch notes), so a bigger drive won't raise that limit. What an M.2 SSD does is give the game and the rest of your library room, so you're not deleting games to fit Skyrim. These come with a built-in heatsink:

    • Samsung 990 PRO with Heatsink (2TB) · Samsung: PS5 compatible
    • WD_BLACK SN850X with Heatsink (2TB) · Sandisk: PS5 (heatsink model)
    • Seagate FireCuda 530 with Heatsink (2TB) · Seagate: PS5 ready

    Each one is a PCIe Gen4 M.2 NVMe drive with a heatsink that its maker lists as PS5 compatible, in line with Sony's PS5 M.2 SSD requirements (Gen4 NVMe, 5,500 MB/s+ read recommended, heatsink required). Update your PS5 system software first, and don't add a second heatsink. Sony also notes USB drives can store PS5 games but can't play them.
