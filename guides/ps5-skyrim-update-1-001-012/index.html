PS5 Skyrim update recap: 1.001.012

This is a short recap of the current Skyrim Special Edition / Anniversary Edition patch the Is It Bricked? tracker is following, and what Bethesda officially listed in the August 2026 notes. It is not a list of which mods broke, and it is not a claim that the update caused any one crash or brick. For player reports, use the mod list.

Last checked 23 November 2026. Official source: Bethesda, "Skyrim Update - August 20", which also includes the 27 August hotfix notes. The tracker lists the current patch as PS5 1.001.012 (27 August 2026). As of that date, no later official Bethesda patch announcement for PS5 Skyrim was found, so the tracker still treats 1.001.012 as current.

Current patch on the tracker: PS5 1.001.012 (27 August 2026)

Bethesda published this as an "Additional Hotfix - August 27" on the same official notes page as the 20 August update. For PlayStation, the hotfix notes say:

  • Fixed a crash when opening the on-screen keyboard and submitting text on the game main menu on PlayStation.
  • Installing the game in certain regions no longer shows the 14 out of 15 GB Creations storage space is used on PlayStation.

The same hotfix also lists an Xbox fix (a crash when opening the map multiple times). Bethesda Support's PlayStation crash article also notes that the on-screen keyboard crash was resolved in a hotfix on 27 August 2026: What do I do if Skyrim is crashing or getting a black screen on PlayStation?

The 20 August 2026 update (1.001.011)

The tracker treats 1.001.012 as a hotfix after this update. Official notes that matter most for PS5 modding include:

  • Increased mod storage to 15 GB for PlayStation 5 and Xbox.
  • Archives are now supported on PlayStation. New file types listed for PlayStation Creations are .nif, .dds, .bink, .wav, and .fuz (with embedded .wav).
  • Bundling of Creations in the Skyrim Creations Menu, to bring it to parity with Fallout and Starfield.
  • Skyrim Creations released through the Verified Creators program can be marked Trophy-Friendly on PlayStation (and Achievement-Friendly on Xbox). Official notes say this requires starting a new game for the functionality to work on updated Creations.
  • A Creation's Details page now includes instructions on how to report abusive or problematic mods.
  • Resolved an issue where Anniversary Edition Creations failed to auto-download on PlayStation 5.
  • Several Creations menu, load-order, and crash fixes, including a crash when the player selects an empty search result in the Creations menu.

The official notes also describe PlayStation 4 storage changes (about 16 GB, with possible download-size vs installed-size differences). This tracker is focused on PS5 reports.

What this page does not claim

Bethesda Support notes that after a game update, Creations may not work properly until they are updated by the creator, and that this can cause issues such as black screens, crashes, and visual UI errors. That is a general warning, not a status for any specific mod.

Player reports on the tracker are from specific setups and may be about load order or conflicts rather than the patch itself. See how we rate mods and the methodology.

If you hit a crash or a brick on this patch, or if a mod started working again, report it.
