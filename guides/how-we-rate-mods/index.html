How we rate mods

Please read: a status is a summary of player reports from specific setups, not a verdict on a mod or its author. Crashes are often caused by load order, missing patches, bundles, or two mods that are each fine on their own, so a label doesn't mean a mod is broken. Please be kind to porters, and check the mod's own page and comments before blaming the last mod you added.

Is It Bricked? is a PS5 Skyrim mod status tracker. We don't test mods ourselves and we don't hand down verdicts. Each status on the mod list is a short summary of public reports we found, and every mod page links those reports so you can read the originals and decide for yourself.

This page is a plain-language version of the same rules used on the methodology page. If the two ever disagree, the methodology page and the sources on the mod page are the ones to trust.

What has to be true before a mod goes on the site

  • There is a firsthand, dated report we can quote and link (usually a player writing about their own game, most often on r/ps5skyrimmods).
  • We open the source in a normal browser when we can, keep quotes word-for-word, and show the date. A date marked ≈ is an estimate, usually from neighbouring posts, and gets replaced once the thread is opened and the date is confirmed.
  • Weak or secondhand claims (hearsay, "I heard this bricks," or a report we can't date or quote) stay off the tracker until a firsthand source shows up.
  • Reader reports sent through the report form are checked against public sources before anything changes. A report without a public link is only used with a clear note that it came to us directly.

The status labels

  • Reported bricks Reports say the game wouldn't start or stayed stuck loading, and fixing it took a reinstall or clearing reserved space.
  • Reported crashes Reports of crashes or freezes during play, with no conflicting "works for me" reports found yet.
  • Mixed reports Credible reports point both ways. We show both sides instead of picking one, because PS5 crashes often depend on load order, PS5 vs PS5 Pro vs PS4, and how you tested. In short: some report problems, some say it works.
  • Works Recent reports of stable use on the current patch, with no unresolved crash reports.
  • Fixed Earlier crash reports, followed by a mod update and new reports that it now works. This label exists in the tracker; as of 23 November 2026 no listed mods are marked Fixed.
  • Unknown Someone suspected a problem, but nobody has confirmed it either way. A single report of a non-crash bug, with no "works" report, is also Unknown. That is how Campfire is handled.

What a status is not

It is not a guarantee that the same thing will happen on your load order. It is not a claim that a porter or author broke the game. If the cause is uncertain — and it often is — we don't blame the mod or the person who ported it. Check the linked reports, the mod's own page, and your load order before you take anything off your list.

As of 23 November 2026 the tracker lists 60 mods: 18 Works, 16 Mixed reports, 11 Unknown, 8 Reported bricks, and 7 Reported crashes.

If something looks wrong, or a mod started working again after an update, tell us.
