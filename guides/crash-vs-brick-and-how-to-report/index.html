What a crash vs a brick means, and how to report a mod

People use "crash" and "brick" to mean different things. On Is It Bricked? the two labels are kept separate, because the player's report is usually about whether the game would even start, or whether it failed during play. This matches the definitions on the methodology page.

Reported crashes

Reported crashes means reports of crashes or freezes during play, with no conflicting "works for me" reports found yet. The game started, then it quit, froze, or otherwise stopped during a session.

Reported bricks

Reported bricks means reports say the game wouldn't start or stayed stuck loading, and fixing it took a reinstall or clearing reserved space. That is a harder failure than a mid-play crash: the player couldn't get back in without rebuilding the install or reserved space.

A mid-play crash that you can recover from by relaunching is not automatically a brick. If a report isn't clear, we don't upgrade it to a brick, and we don't blame a mod or its porter when the cause is uncertain.

Why your result may not match the tracker

A status is a summary of player reports from specific setups, not a verdict on a mod or its author. Crashes are often caused by load order, missing patches, bundles, or two mods that are each fine on their own. PS5, PS5 Pro, and PS4 can also differ. See how we rate mods.

Tips the community repeats, from the methodology page:

  • Add mods a few at a time, then fully close and relaunch the game. Some crashes only show up on the second launch.
  • Test on a fresh save, and don't add or remove mods partway through a playthrough.
  • Use a quick-start test mod such as Realm of Lorkhan.

Bethesda Support also notes that after a game update, Creations may not work properly until they are updated by the creator. Their PlayStation crash article is here: Skyrim crashing or black screen on PlayStation.

How to report a mod

If a mod works, crashes, bricks your install, or starts working again after an update, use the report form, or email isitbricked@gmail.com.

The most useful reports include:

  • The exact mod name, and a Creations link if you have one.
  • Your platform (PS5, PS5 Pro, or PS4) and, if you know it, the game version or the date you tested (the tracker is currently following PS5 1.001.012).
  • What you did and what you saw, in your own words. Mention your load order if it might matter.
  • A public source link if the same report is already on Reddit or elsewhere.

Every status on this site is backed by a source. Reports are checked against public posts before the tracker changes, so it may take a few days to show up. A report without a public link is only used with a clear note that it came to us directly.
