Is Sacrosanct safe on PS5?

Short answer: Unknown Not confirmed either way yet for Sacrosanct.

Based on 1 player report; may be setup-specific.

Please read: a status is a summary of player reports from specific setups, not a verdict on a mod or its author. Crashes are often caused by load order, missing patches, bundles, or two mods that are each fine on their own, so a label doesn't mean a mod is broken.
