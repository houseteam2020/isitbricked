Is Nordskog 2K safe on PS5?

Short answer: Works Players report that Nordskog 2K works on PS5.

Based on 2 player reports; may be setup-specific.

Please read: a status is a summary of player reports from specific setups, not a verdict on a mod or its author. Crashes are often caused by load order, missing patches, bundles, or two mods that are each fine on their own, so a label doesn't mean a mod is broken. Please be kind to porters, and check the mod's own page and comments before blaming the last mod you added.

Last checked 2026-11-23 · Based on 2 dated player reports (2 works), latest 2026-11-19 · Current game patch: PS5 1.001.012 (2026-08-27, notes) · Category: Trees / Flora

One player spent a few hours testing Nordskog 2K on a PS5 Pro, including with extra tree-placement mods, and said performance stayed smooth in almost every area, with only minor micro-stutters in Riverwood and during some cell transitions. A day later they were about 40 hours into a playthrough using their Nordskog setup (later with Happy Little Trees as well) and said everything had been running smoothly.

Works means: Recent reports of stable use on the current patch, with no unresolved crash reports.
