Is Unofficial Skyrim Special Edition Patch (USSEP) safe on PS5?

Short answer: Works Players report that Unofficial Skyrim Special Edition Patch (USSEP) works on PS5.

Last checked 2026-10-20 · Based on 2 dated player reports (2 works), latest 2026-09-05 · Current game patch: PS5 1.001.012 (2026-08-27, notes) · Category: Bug fixes
