Is Children of the Wave safe on PS5?

Short answer: Reported crashes Players report that Children of the Wave crashes or freezes the game on PS5.

Based on 1 player report; may be setup-specific.

Please read: a status is a summary of player reports from specific setups, not a verdict on a mod or its author. Crashes are often caused by load order, missing patches, bundles, or two mods that are each fine on their own, so a label doesn't mean a mod is broken. Please be kind to porters, and check the mod's own page and comments before blaming the last mod you added.

Last checked 2026-11-23 · Based on 1 dated player report (1 crash), latest 2026-11-19 · Current game patch: PS5 1.001.012 (2026-08-27, notes) · Category: NPCs / Quest

One player says Children of the Wave instantly crashes the game when entering Fort Dawnguard, and that removing it stopped the crash. They also tried other mods in the same "Children of" series (Dawns Beauty, the Ash, the First, and the Green) and said those worked.

Reported crashes means: Reports of crashes or freezes during play, with no conflicting "works for me" reports found yet.
