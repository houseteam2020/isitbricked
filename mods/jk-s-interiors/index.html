Is JK's Interiors safe on PS5?

Short answer: Mixed reports Player reports on JK's Interiors are mixed: some report problems, some say it works.

Based on 2 player reports; may be setup-specific.

Please read: a status is a summary of player reports from specific setups, not a verdict on a mod or its author. Crashes are often caused by load order, missing patches, bundles, or two mods that are each fine on their own, so a label doesn't mean a mod is broken. Please be kind to porters, and check the mod's own page and comments before blaming the last mod you added.

Last checked 2026-11-16 · Based on 2 dated player reports (1 works, 1 unknown), latest 2026-11-07 · Current game patch: PS5 1.001.012 (2026-08-27, notes) · Category: Interiors

One player testing with only USSEP, Simple Workaround Framework and one other mod found an NPC added by JK's Interiors had a black face. Another player uses JK's Interiors and says it has some other problems but is worth it. No crashes reported.

Mixed reports means: Credible reports point both ways, so we show both sides instead of picking one.
